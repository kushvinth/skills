# Notice

Xiaohei Illustrations is an AI-agent skill for generating hand-drawn Chinese article illustrations.

The bundled example images in `examples/images/` and `xiaohei-illustrations/assets/examples/` are included as style calibration examples for this skill.

The recurring character "小黑" is part of this skill's visual language for Chinese article illustrations. When redistributing or adapting this repository, please keep the `Xiaohei Illustrations` name in your derived documentation.
